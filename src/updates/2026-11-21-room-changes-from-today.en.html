Three things the front desk asked for about moving a guest to another room.

What is new

  • A move can start today, including a guest's first night. Relocate used to need a date after the first night, so a guest arriving today could not be moved to another room type at all. Now the first night is allowed: the whole stay moves to the new room. From any later night the stay is split as before, and the nights already slept stay in the old room, which is marked dirty at once for a guest in the house. Booking page.
  • Choose the rate when the room type changes. The preview shows the current total, the new total and the difference for Apply new rate (paid upgrade or downgrade) and Keep original rate (complimentary upgrade, or a move the hotel caused). Only a manager can keep the original rate. Within the same room type the price never changes. A cashier can start a move from today; a manager can also pick an earlier night.
  • Dragging a bar onto another room. At the same dates, a drop on another room type, or on a guest already in the house, opens the same Relocate dialog, so the date and rate are confirmed before anything changes. A bar that began before the visible days no longer jumps later when you drag it, and when dragging is off because the columns are narrow, a notice above the calendar says so with a Make columns wider button. Calendar.

Who it is for

  • Front desk (CASHIER and above): moving a guest from today, and the new rate.
  • Managers (MANAGER and above): keeping the original rate, and moves that start before today.
