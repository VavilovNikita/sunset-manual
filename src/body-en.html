The Sunset Beach Resort & Spa · Staff handbook

Sunset system user manual

How to run the hotel in the Sunset system: the front desk, rooms, the restaurant POS, the spa, the gym, staff schedules, Telegram notifications and the reports. Every screen is shown as it looks today, with the rules the system enforces and the reasons behind them.

Version: October 2026Audience: front desk, cashiers, waiters, spa, engineering, managers, administratorsAll names and figures in the screenshots are demo data

1Getting started

The parts of the system

Sunset is one system with several doors. Every door reads and writes the same data on the hotel's server, so a booking made at the desk is on the calendar, the property map and the night audit at once.

DoorAddressWho uses it
Office admin/admin on the hotel websiteFront desk, managers, administrators, spa reception. Desktop or laptop with a mouse.
Floor POS/posWaiters and cashiers on a phone or tablet that is passed between staff.
Staff app (Android)Installed from the hotel's own serverThe same staff functions in a native app: Today, calendar, bookings, POS, shift, spa, housekeeping, maintenance, reports.
Staff Telegram botLinked per employeeEach employee's own schedule, shift requests, alerts for directors, task and spa screens.
Public website and guest account/, /guestGuests: room search and booking requests, account, room service, gym booking, ordering from the table by QR code.

The office screens are in English. The Telegram bot speaks English, Thai and Russian.

Roles and job functions

Every staff account has exactly one role. Roles are a ladder: each one can do everything the role below it can.

RoleTypical personWhat it adds
WAITERWaiter, bartender, any staff memberTake orders, send them to the kitchen, print pre-bills, report a room problem, see own schedule.
CASHIERFront desk, cashierBookings, check-in and check-out, folio payments, closing orders, cash shift, spa and gym bookings, housekeeping, night audit.
MANAGERDuty manager, F&B managerMenu, tables, printers, prices and room blocks, voids, roster, reports, audit history, lighting, a negotiated price on a new booking, back-dated bookings.
ADMINOwner, ITUsers and passwords, settings pages (guest emails, print layouts, notifications, gym and lighting devices), schedule import.

A job function is a second, separate label. A person can hold none, one or several, whatever their role. A function never moves someone up the role ladder.

  • ENGINEER starts and closes maintenance tasks (a manager can too).
  • HOUSEKEEPER can be named as responsible for a room block.
  • THERAPIST can be booked for spa treatments and reads the spa schedule, even as a WAITER.
  • HR (one active person at most) is the HR director: decides shift requests and reads the roster and attendance.
Rule

The system hides what a role cannot do, and the server refuses it as well. A link that is not in your menu is not a missing feature. Opening such a page directly shows Not available for your role.

Signing in and the menu

Staff sign in at /admin/login with email and password. After sign-in each person lands on their own starting page: waiters on the POS table board, cashiers on Today, engineers on Maintenance, therapists on the Spa schedule, managers and administrators on the Dashboard.

The left menu has seven groups: Front desk, Rooms, Restaurant, Spa, Gym & lighting, Staff, Reports. On a desktop, hover a group to open it; under a thin divider each group lists its own Settings links. On a tablet or phone the groups open with a tap.

  • Five wrong passwords for the same email from the same place lock sign-in for 15 minutes. The message is the same whether the account exists or not.
  • A session lasts 7 days. Changing a password, an administrator resetting it, a role change or disabling the account signs that person out everywhere at once.
  • The floor POS signs out after 30 minutes without a touch, and Not you? Switch signs out on demand, because the phone changes hands.

Conventions used everywhere

  • The server calculates every amount. Prices, totals, balances and report figures come from the server. The only figure a screen works out for itself is the change to give a guest paying cash.
  • Hotel time. "Today" is the hotel's day in Bangkok time, whatever the clock of the computer says. Dates read like 24 Oct 2026, times like 24 Oct 2026, 14:05.
  • Colours. Sea green means free or available. Coral means something needs action (blocked, oversold, overdue). Amber means attention but not urgent (money owed, no room assigned). Green means paid.
  • Errors appear next to the button that caused them. If a list fails to reload, the last data stays on screen with a message.
  • Everything important is in History. Money, bookings, prices, users and settings changes are recorded with who did it and when (see History).

2Front desk

Today board

Front desk → Today is the desk's to-do list for the hotel day: who is arriving, who is leaving, and who is in the house. It lists only open work; a guest who has already checked in disappears from Arriving today.

Check in

  1. Find the guest under Arriving today and press Check in.
  2. If the room is not marked clean, a dialog says so. Press Check in anyway if the guest accepts, or Back to wait for housekeeping.
  3. The card moves to In-house. The check-in is recorded in the booking's history.

Check-in is refused for a cancelled booking, before the arrival date, and when the guest is already checked in. A booking with no room assigned cannot be checked in until a room is chosen. A prepaid (PAID) booking checks in like any other.

Check out

  1. Press Check out on the guest's card.
  2. If money is still owed, the dialog shows Balance due now. Record the payment in the folio first (see Folio), or press Check out anyway: the debt stays on the booking and appears in the night audit under Unpaid balances.
  3. The room is marked dirty for housekeeping.

Early departure. When a guest leaves before the booked date, the booking page offers to shorten the stay to today. Staff choose each time whether the released nights are still charged (an early-departure fee) or released. If the stay was moved between rooms, rooms the guest never reached are dropped.

No-show. No-show marks a guest who never came. A no-show's room is not counted as owed; other charges still are.

Booking calendar

Front desk → Calendar shows every physical room as a row and every night as a column. The number under each room type is how many rooms of that type are still free that night. A dot next to a room number means the room is not clean.

  • Period: ← Prev, Today, Next →, the quick picks (This month, Next 3 months, Year), or type From and To and press Apply. Up to 366 days at once.
  • Density makes columns wider or narrower. Dragging needs columns wide enough to hit a night exactly.
  • Create: drag across free nights on a room's row, or click a free cell, to open the new-booking form.
  • Change dates: drag a bar's edge. Move: drag the whole bar to another room or date. A drop on another booking in a different room offers a swap of the two rooms (same room type only). Every drag asks for confirmation and shows the new price first.
  • Open: double-click a bar (tap on a touchscreen) to open the booking panel.
Business rules for new bookings
  • A stay is at most 90 nights.
  • A booking that starts before today (back entry) needs a MANAGER.
  • More guests than the room's capacity is accepted from staff with a warning (an extra bed is real). The website refuses it.
  • Set price manually (MANAGER and above): type a negotiated total for the whole stay. The server spreads it over the nights and the history records both the typed and the rate-plan price. A cashier does not see this link.
  • Purpose Complimentary or House use marks a free or staff stay for the reports.

Bookings list

Front desk → Bookings searches all bookings by guest name, email, phone or reference, with date and status filters. Click a column heading to sort. Export CSV (MANAGER and above) downloads the list with the room, balance due and creation time.

Booking status

StatusMeaning
NEWA request, usually from the website. Not yet confirmed.
CONFIRMEDThe hotel has accepted the booking.
PAIDThe room is fully paid. Setting PAID requires recording the payment of the remaining amount at the same moment (cash, card or other, e.g. bank transfer or OTA prepayment). PAID never writes money off.
CANCELLEDCancelled, with a reason. A guest who has already arrived cannot be cancelled: check them out or shorten the stay instead.

Separately from status, each booking has an occupancy state: expected, checked in, checked out or no-show. A booking is never deleted, only cancelled.

Website requests expire

A NEW request from the website that nobody confirms within 2 business days is cancelled automatically, and managers get a reminder before that. Bookings made by staff never expire.

Booking page

Open a booking from the list, the calendar or Today. The page holds everything about the stay.

  • Dates & room → Change: new dates or another room. Preview price shows the server's new total before you confirm.
  • Prices are frozen per night. The price agreed for a night stays. Extending a stay prices only the new nights at today's rates; earlier nights keep their price. Reprice to current rates is a separate, explicit action, and only for nights from today on.
  • Relocate to another room: move the guest from a chosen date, to the same or another room type. The preview shows the new total and the difference. The booking then has two room segments; Undo this relocation restores the original room and prices.
  • Payment note: for a receipt or transfer reference. Never type a card number: a note that looks like one is refused.
  • Linked to ties the booking to a guest card (see Guests).

Folio and payments

The Folio is the one place that answers "what does this guest owe?". The same figure is shown on Today, the property map, check-out and the night audit.

  • Room: the agreed price of the nights (plus any early-departure fee).
  • POS room charges: restaurant, bar and spa bills charged to the room.
  • Paid at the desk: folio payments already recorded.
  • Balance due = charges − payments.
  1. Under Balance due, the amount field is filled with the full balance. Change it for a part payment.
  2. Choose the method: Cash, Card or Other.
  3. Press Record payment. Cash goes into your open cash shift. When the room is fully covered, the booking becomes PAID by itself.

Payments are applied to POS charges first, then to the room. A future unpaid booking does not "owe" yet; the Owes badge appears only once the guest is in the house, has left, the dates are over, or the booking was cancelled with charges left.

Accounting

Every folio payment, closed POS order and PAID settlement is posted to the hotel's double-entry ledger in the same moment. A mistake is corrected by a reversing entry, never by editing.

Guests

Front desk → Guests is the guest card index. A card holds contact details, the stay history with amounts and the automated emails sent. New guest creates a card; a card can be edited or deleted. Has account (verified) means the guest registered on the website.

On a booking, Change next to Linked to attaches a different card and Unlink removes it. Bookings imported from SiteMinder always get a new card with the name only; the system never matches guests by name, because a wrong match would show the stay in a stranger's account.

Property map

Front desk → Property map places every room on the hotel's plan and colours it by what is true right now: vacant and ready, guest in the house, blocked, out of service. Small dots add owes money, not cleaned and arriving or leaving today. A coral ring means action is needed (an overdue guest, a booked room that is blocked).

In house

The list of occupied rooms for a night, with guest, adults and children, segment (OTA, direct, walk-in…), arrival and departure. A guest still checked in after their departure date is listed and flagged overdue: they hold the room until they check out.

Night audit

Front desk → Night audit reviews a day before closing it. The cards show room-nights sold, occupancy and the agreed room revenue for that night. Below are the lists of unresolved items, including ones left over from earlier days:

  • Missed arrivals: still expected on or before this date. Check them in or mark no-show.
  • Not arrived — mark no-show or cancel: dates are over and the guest never came.
  • Missed departures: still checked in on or after their departure date. Staying unpaid · N nights counts nights past check-out with nothing agreed. Check out, or extend the stay (which prices the extra nights).
  • Unpaid balances: finished stays that still owe money.

Guest emails (ADMIN)

Front desk → Settings → Guest emails switches on three automated emails, sent once a day at 10:00: Pre-arrival (N days before check-in), Post-stay (N days after check-out, with an optional review link) and Win-back (N months after the last stay). They go only to guests with a verified website account who have not unsubscribed, never to a card typed in at the desk.

SiteMinder (OTA) import

Reservations from Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia and other channels arrive through SiteMinder. A small program on the office PC reads SiteMinder every night and sends each reservation to Sunset. Nothing is sent back to SiteMinder.

  • A new reservation creates a booking with SiteMinder's total as the agreed price, after the same availability check as any booking. If there is no room, the import is refused and a person must resolve the overbooking.
  • A changed reservation updates the dates or party through the normal paths; a cancelled one cancels the booking and releases the room.
  • SiteMinder room type names are mapped to Sunset room types by a manager. An unknown name is refused, never guessed.
  • Such bookings show SiteMinder and the reference under the guest's name. In History they appear with the actor SITEMINDER.

3Rooms

Housekeeping

Rooms → Housekeeping lists every room with its status code: VC vacant clean, VD vacant dirty, OC occupied clean, OD occupied dirty, plus Due out today, Arriving today, Overdue or Out of order. Press Mark clean or Mark dirty.

Maintenance

Rooms → Maintenance is open to every staff member: whoever notices a problem reports it. Press + Report a problem, choose the room, describe the problem and add photos.

  • A manager can block the room from a task (dates, reason, who closes it: engineer or housekeeper). A blocked room is out of sale on those dates.
  • A block is created even when a guest is booked in the room, with a warning naming the booking: the broken air conditioner is a fact, and the desk decides what to do with the guest.
  • Closing a task shortens its block to end yesterday at the latest, so the room returns to sale; a block that already ended is not extended.
  • The engineer can do the same from Telegram (Tasks screen).

Rates & availability

Rooms → Rates & availability is one table: room types down, days of the month across.

  • A manager clicks a price, types a new one and presses Enter (Esc cancels).
  • Set a price range: room type, from, to, price per night, Apply to range. Up to 366 days at once.
  • New prices apply to new bookings and new nights only. Nights already agreed on existing bookings keep their price.

A room block (temporarily out of sale) and an inactive room (permanently out of service) are two different things. A blocked room is still an active room.

Room types and physical rooms

Rooms → Settings → Rooms lists room types. A manager creates or edits a type (name, description, capacity, base price per night, photos) and, under it, the physical rooms of that type with their labels (101, 102…). A room that has history cannot be deleted; deactivate it instead.

Room type names and room labels must be unique; "Garden Villa" and "garden villa" count as the same name.

4Restaurant POS

Tables board and floor map

Restaurant → POS (admin) and /pos (phone) show every table by zone. A table with an order shows its status and total. Open tickets (No table on the phone) holds orders without a table, such as takeaway or a guest at the pool bar.

Taking an order

  1. Tap a table. Nothing is created yet: the order exists only when the first item is added.
  2. Choose a category and tap items; each tap adds one. Use ✎ to add an item with a note ("no ice", "medium spicy").
  3. Adjust with − / + or Remove while the line has not been sent.
  4. Press Send to kitchen. Kitchen items print on the kitchen printer, drinks on the bar printer. The button stays in place as Sent ✓.
  5. More items can be added to a sent order; they are sent and printed when added.
Rules
  • A line is 1 to 99 pieces.
  • A line that was sent is voided, never edited. Void needs a MANAGER and a reason; a VOID ticket prints at the same station.
  • Cancelling an order with sent lines also needs a manager and a reason. An order that was never sent can be cancelled by the waiter.
  • An order with no items cannot be closed. Empty orders left open are cancelled automatically when the shift closes.
  • Prices include VAT 7%. The receipt shows "incl. VAT" as a breakdown, never as an addition. There is no service charge.

Print pre-bill prints the bill for the guest to check. Print QR prints a code the guest scans to see the bill and order more from their phone.

Taking payment

Closing an order needs a CASHIER with an open cash shift. Without one the payment block says Open a shift to accept payment with a link.

  • Card records a card payment. Sunset is not connected to the card terminal; the cashier confirms the terminal approved it.
  • Room charges the order to a guest's folio. Search by guest name; only guests currently checked in are offered (an overdue guest is still checked in). The guest pays it at check-out.
  • A closed order prints a guest receipt on the cashier printer, if one is set up.
  • An order is paid once, in full. Splitting a bill into several payments is not supported.

The phone POS

The phone screens do the same work with large buttons. Before any money action the phone shows Will be recorded as with the signed-in person, because the phone is shared. If it is the wrong person, use Not you? Switch.

Cash shifts

A shift in the POS means one cashier's session at the till, from opening the drawer to counting it. (Work schedules are called roster, and clock-ins attendance.)

  1. Restaurant → Shifts: type the Opening cash float and press Open shift.
  2. During the shift the cards show Cash, Card, Room charge and the number of payments.
  3. To close, count the drawer and type Counted cash. The screen compares it with Expected cash (float + cash sales + cash folio payments) and shows the Discrepancy.
  4. If the drawer does not match, Notes are required. Then press Close shift (on the phone: Review & close, then Confirm close). A Z-report prints.

A shift is blocked only by orders that this cashier opened, or that carry a payment in this shift. Room charges are never cash in the drawer: they are debt moved to the guest's folio.

Printers and the print queue

Kitchen tickets, bar tickets, pre-bills, receipts and Z-reports go to network printers. Each department (Kitchen, Bar, Cashier) has at most one active printer.

  • Printing never stops the work. If a printer does not answer, the job waits in the queue and is retried automatically (5 attempts by default), then marked Failed.
  • Not printed = Failed + Pending. The banner on the tables board and the queue's default tab use this one rule.
  • Retry sends exactly the document that was queued, not a fresh one. Fix the printer first if its address changed. Dismiss hides a job you have dealt with.

Menu

Restaurant → Menu: everyone can read it, a manager edits it. Each item has a name, category (how it is grouped on screen), department (which printer gets the ticket: Kitchen or Bar), price and an Available switch. Spa treatments are kept separately under Spa → Treatments.

5Spa

Appointments

Spa → Appointments: rows are spa tables, columns are 30-minute slots from 09:00 to 20:00. Click a free slot to book; drag an appointment to another table or time; double-click it to open its panel.

Rules the server checks
  • The guest must have a booking that is in the house or expected, and the date must be within the stay.
  • The therapist must be an active employee with the THERAPIST function; the table must be in the spa zone.
  • No two appointments may overlap on one table or for one therapist. The form greys out busy therapists, but the server has the final word.
  • A treatment never starts in the past; today the earliest is the slot under way.
  • The length of an appointment is the sum of its treatments. Add or remove a treatment to change it; there is no stretching by drag.

Billing a treatment

  1. In the panel press Bill treatment. An order opens on the Spa tab with the appointment's guest already selected.
  2. Close it like any order: cash, card, or charge to the room.
  3. When the appointment is Completed, a warning ⚠ appears if any treatment is not on a paid or open order. Add to the order adds exactly what is missing.

Prices are always the current menu price at the moment a treatment is billed. If a booking is cancelled, its future spa appointments are cancelled too and unpaid spa lines are removed; anything already paid is left alone and a warning appears on the booking. A stay that gets shorter cancels appointments that fall outside it.

6Gym and lighting

Gym schedule

The gym takes one guest at a time. Gym & lighting → Gym schedule shows days as rows and half-hour slots as columns (Day or Week view). Click a free slot to reserve it for a hotel booking; open a reservation to move, shorten or cancel it.

  • Sessions are 30 to 120 minutes in 30-minute steps, within the opening hours; at most 120 minutes a day per booking.
  • Guests book themselves on the website only while checked in. Staff can also book an expected guest.
  • When a reservation starts, the fans and air conditioners selected for bookings switch on a few minutes earlier and switch off at the end, unless the next session follows.
  • Check-out, cancellation or a shorter stay cancels the sessions that no longer fit.

Lighting

Lighting (MANAGER+) switches the WLED lighting controllers on the hotel network: on/off, brightness and stored presets. Brightness 0% means off. The page does not refresh by itself; press Refresh. Lighting devices (ADMIN) adds controllers by their local address and checks the connection. Only addresses inside the hotel network that the server allows can be added.

Every employee sees their own month under Staff → My schedule: the shift code and times for each day.

Attendance rules
  • The fingerprint terminal is read by the server every few minutes. In and out are decided by order (first scan in, next out…), not by the button pressed on the terminal. A second scan within 2 minutes is ignored as a double tap.
  • A punch is never edited or deleted. A correction replaces the whole day: the old scans stay in history as voided, the new times are recorded with a required reason.
  • If a terminal stops answering, late and absence alerts are suppressed, because without it "late" would be an accusation.

Fingerprint terminals (MANAGER+) registers terminals by address; Resync re-reads the full log. Import schedule (ADMIN) reads the hotel's hand-made Excel schedule or re-imports an edited grid export; names are mapped once by a person and existing entries are never overwritten.

Shift requests

Employees ask in the Telegram bot to move a shift to another day or to take a day off. The HR director decides, either in Telegram or under Staff → Shift requests.

  • Approving changes the roster at once, with every roster check. If the shift changed since the request was filed, the request becomes Expired and nothing changes.
  • Coverage below the minimum is shown as a warning; approving still works.
  • While nobody holds HR, or the HR director is on leave, administrators decide. The HR director's own requests always go to administrators.
  • Managers who are not today's deciders see the list without buttons. The HR director without a manager role sees the roster read only.
  • The HR director's leave is set here (Save leave) or in the bot.

Users

Staff → Settings → Users (ADMIN) lists staff by department. Each row has the role, functions (Engineer, Housekeeper, Therapist, HR), fingerprint number, password reset and disable. A person can exist without a login (only on the roster); tick Can sign in to give them email and password.

An administrator cannot change their own role or disable themselves. A deactivated employee gets no new shifts, disappears from the Today board and loses the Telegram link; their history stays.

9Reports

Dashboard

Room money on the dashboard is money actually recorded at the desk (folio payments), never a sum of booking prices. Room charges are shown separately because they are not money in hand until the guest pays at check-out.

Reports

Reports → Reports (MANAGER+) has one tab per report. Room reports count every night of every booking that is not cancelled; room revenue is the agreed price, not money collected.

TabShows

A guest still checked in after their departure date counts as occupied in every report, with no revenue for the extra nights.

History (audit log)

Reports → History (MANAGER+) is a read-only record of significant actions: bookings, payments, prices, orders and voids, shifts, users, settings, roster and attendance corrections. Filter by staff email, action, entity and dates. Actions done by the system itself (for example the expiry of a website request) are shown as System; actions done through Telegram are marked "via Telegram".

10The guest side

Staff should know what guests see, because guests will ask about it.

  • Website booking. A guest picks dates, sees the server's price for the whole stay and sends a request. It arrives as NEW and needs confirming at the desk within 2 business days. Up to eight attempts per hour per visitor.
  • Guest account. Registration with email verification. The account shows the guest's bookings and lets them order room service and book the gym while checked in.
  • Room service and table QR. The guest orders from their phone. The order appears on the POS like any other and prints in the kitchen and bar.

12What the system deliberately does not do

  • Delete a booking. Bookings are cancelled with a reason, never deleted.
  • Split a bill. An order is paid once, in full.
  • Talk to the card terminal. "Card" is the cashier's statement that the terminal approved the payment.
  • Recalculate agreed prices silently. Nights already agreed keep their price; repricing is an explicit action.
  • Write money off. PAID requires a recorded payment; checking out with a balance leaves a visible debt.
  • Edit history. Payments, ledger entries, fingerprint punches and the audit log are corrected by new records, never by editing old ones.
  • Work offline. Without a connection, a screen says so and nothing pretends to be saved.
  • Push changes back to SiteMinder. The import is one-way.
