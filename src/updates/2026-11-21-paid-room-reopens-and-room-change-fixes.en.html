Fixes found while checking the room changes of the previous update.

What changed

  • A Room paid booking no longer hides a higher price. Moving a guest to a dearer room at the new rate, extending the stay or repricing used to show a settled folio with nothing to pay. Now the preview warns (Room paid will be reopened: the guest will owe ฿X), the booking goes back to Confirmed, and the difference shows as a balance due. Recording the payment marks the room paid again by itself. If the price goes down the room stays paid and the difference is a credit. What was already paid is never charged twice, including a room marked paid by hand before payments were recorded. Booking page.
  • Cashiers choose the room when relocating. The room picker was hidden from them, and an in-house guest could be sent to "no room assigned". Everyone who can move a guest now picks the room, and a guest in the house must be given one.
  • Readable history on the booking page. It showed raw values such as status CONFIRMED; it now reads like the calendar panel (Confirmed, Tentative, Room paid).
  • Calendar drops never end in nothing. Dragging a booking across other bookings to a free night used to cancel the drop silently; it now moves there. A drop between rooms, or a swap between two room types, says on the page what happened. The hint above the calendar now says to double-click a booking (tap on a touchscreen) to open it. Calendar.
  • Small things. The Future bookings CSV names the channel as on screen (Phone, not PHONE); the room-move dialog's title sits inside its frame; Attendance columns have the same width for every day.

Who it is for

  • Front desk (CASHIER and above): choosing the room when moving a guest, the warning on a paid room.
  • Managers (MANAGER and above): keeping the original rate, which changes no price and so leaves a paid room paid.
A paid room

The previous update said a higher total on a Room paid booking only showed as a balance due, and that nothing was posted to the ledger. That is replaced: the booking is reopened, and the ledger follows (the room settlement is reversed and posted again when the booking is paid again).
