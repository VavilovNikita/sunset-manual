Four changes to everyday booking work.

What is new

  • Tentative / Confirmed. The new-booking form has two switches, Tentative and Confirmed (Confirmed is preselected). The status list now reads Tentative where it used to say NEW, in the Bookings list, the calendar, the booking page and the history. Nothing else about statuses changed. Booking status.
  • Notes on a booking. An optional field when you create a booking, and a Notes card on the booking page and in the calendar panel: airport pickup, allergies, special requests. A note icon appears in the Bookings list and on arrivals on Today. The history says that notes changed, never what they say. Guests never see them. Booking page.
  • Future bookings report. Reports has a new tab: who arrives in a date range, by status (Tentative, Confirmed, Room paid), with counts, nights, room price, balance due and notes. Export CSV downloads it. Future bookings.
  • Roster. The row of dates and the employee column now stay in view while you scroll the grid (and the Attendance table). Roster.

Who it is for

  • Front desk (CASHIER and above): choosing the status when creating a booking, and the notes.
  • Managers and administrators (MANAGER and above): the Future bookings report and its CSV; the roster.
Nothing changes for guests

The website and the guest account keep their own wording, and notes are never sent to the guest. A website request that nobody confirms still expires after 2 business days; a booking staff enter as Tentative does not expire.
