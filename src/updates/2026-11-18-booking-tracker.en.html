The "SBR Booking Tracker" workbook no longer has to be typed in by hand. Sunset works out the same tables from the live bookings, shows them in the admin and exports them to Excel.

What is new

  • Booking tracker tab in Reports: Results by month against a target and a baseline, Channel summary with charts, Booking trends (booking window and 12-week pace) and a Weekly review that is snapshotted automatically every Friday at 23:55. Export xlsx downloads it as an Excel file. Read how it is calculated.
  • Breakfast on the booking: guests per night and a rate of ฿250 or ฿350. It is only taken out of the room price in the tracker; nothing is charged to the guest. Booking page.
  • New channels: Ctrip / Trip.com, Hotelbeds and Travel agent. In the Market segment report all three count as OTA. Reports.
  • Booked on on the booking page: for SiteMinder bookings it is SiteMinder's own booking date. SiteMinder import.
  • Tax rates (Reports → Settings): the service charge and VAT percentages the tracker takes out of room prices. Tax rates.

Who it is for

  • Managers and administrators: the Booking tracker tab, targets and baseline, weekly review notes and the Excel export (MANAGER and above).
  • Front desk (CASHIER and above): breakfast and the new channels on a booking.
  • Administrators: Tax rates (ADMIN).
Nothing changes for guests

The service charge and VAT in the tracker are a deduction for the analysis only. No guest bill, folio, receipt or POS total changes.
